The idea behind Proofstone

Everything Proofstone makes rests on three statements. Here's the reason for each.

Step Statements Reasons
1 Every step comes with a reason. Students hold on to math that makes sense to them.
2 Wrong answers get explained, not just marked. A mistake a student understands is one they stop making.
3 The adult in the room gets support too. A good math class shouldn't depend on having a math specialist on staff.

About me

I'm Matt Byers, a math teacher and curriculum designer based in Louisville, Kentucky.

My first classroom was in China, right after I finished my physics degree at Indiana University. In 2020 I moved to Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia where I joined the Canadian School of Ulaanbaatar as a founding faculty member. There I built the school's grades 9–12 math curriculum from scratch, through AP Calculus, bringing together Mongolia's national standards and the curriculum of New Brunswick, Canada.

I hold an M.Ed. in Educational Technology from Moreland University and Kentucky & D.C. teaching licenses in mathematics. I design and code Proofstone's interactive lessons myself, and you can try one of them.
