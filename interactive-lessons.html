Proofstone builds interactive lessons that take one idea students struggle with and let them rebuild it step by step: change the figure, answer a question, see why it's true. They run in any browser on a laptop, tablet or phone, with nothing to install and no student accounts.

What's in every lesson

A figure students control
Students change the math and watch what happens, instead of reading about it.
A question at each step
The next step opens only after students answer, so nobody skims past the idea.
A note on every wrong answer
Each wrong choice explains what went wrong, so students can fix their thinking and try again.
A result built as they go
Students finish having derived the formula, not been handed it.
Nothing to install
Any modern browser on a laptop, tablet or phone, with no student accounts.

What makes a good topic

Ideas that are easier to see than to say. Formulas for area and volume, slope and rate of change, transformations, the unit circle, completing the square, and why the rules for exponents or the distributive property work all fit this format well.

How a custom lesson gets made

  1. You tell me the idea, the grade level and where students get stuck.
  2. I send a short plan: the figure, the steps and the questions. Nothing gets built until you approve it.
  3. I build the lesson and test it on laptops, tablets and phones.
  4. You try it, I make revisions, and you get a link to share with students.
