Find the mistake

Here's a worked solution written the way AI tools write them: tidy, confident and even checked. One line is wrong. Which one?

Step Statements Reasons
1 x² = 3x Given
2 x = 3 Divide both sides by x.
3 3² = 9 and 3 · 3 = 9 Substitute x = 3 to check.
∴Therefore The only solution is x = 3.∎

Which line is wrong?

This one is simple on purpose. The same slip, dividing by something that might be zero, hides in trig and rational equations, and AI tools make it without any warning.

What teachers learn

The training covers three areas, weighted toward what your teachers need most.

Plan lessons and make materials faster

How to use AI to draft lesson plans, practice sets, leveled versions of a worksheet and exit tickets, with prompts that get usable math out of it instead of generic filler.

Catch AI's math mistakes

Where AI tools tend to go wrong in math, like slips in long calculations, lost solutions, answer keys that don't match their problems and word problems with impossible numbers, plus a quick routine for checking anything before it reaches students.

Set rules for student AI use

Clear rules for when students can use AI in math class and how they should show it, plus ways to design homework and tests that still show what each student understands.
