The courses

Each is a full school year, aligned to the Common Core State Standards.

Pre-Algebra
Rational numbers, exponents and scientific notation, linear equations, proportional relationships and a first look at functions.
Algebra 1
Linear equations and inequalities, functions, systems of equations, exponential growth and decay, and quadratics.
Geometry
Transformations, congruence and similarity, proof, right-triangle trigonometry, circles, and area and volume.
Algebra 2
Polynomial, rational, exponential and logarithmic functions, trigonometric functions, and statistics.

Questions schools ask

Why not use a free curriculum?
Free curricula give you the math. This gives the adult teaching it a video lesson and a script for each day, plus answer keys that explain the mistakes.
Is it built on existing material?
Partly. It adapts an openly licensed curriculum, credited in the materials, and adds everything a non-specialist needs to teach it.
Is it aligned to standards?
Yes. Every course is aligned to the Common Core State Standards.
What if only two students need it?
Buy two seats. There's no minimum.
